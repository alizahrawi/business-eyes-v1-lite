# Business Eyes Product Vision

## Vision

Business Eyes helps organizations turn everyday operational activity into structured, measurable, and decision-ready intelligence.

The product family is built around a simple principle:

> People should be able to work naturally while the system creates the structure required for management, analysis, and continuous improvement.

## Product principles

1. **Natural input, structured output** — users should not need to learn prompt engineering or complex reporting standards.
2. **Manager-ready information** — outputs should support evaluation and decision-making, not merely document activity.
3. **Measurable operations** — time, actions, outcomes, and workflow context should become usable business data.
4. **Modular products** — each Business Eyes capability should have a clear boundary and remain independently maintainable.
5. **Secure by default** — authentication, access, payment, and AI usage controls belong in the backend.
6. **Persian-first experience** — the initial product experience is designed for Persian-speaking users and teams.

## Current milestone

Business Eyes v1 Lite begins with the `Daily Report Builder` Telegram Mini App. It validates the core product idea: transforming natural daily notes into standardized professional reports with minimal friction.
